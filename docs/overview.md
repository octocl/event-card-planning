# Product overview

## Confirmed direction

**EventCard** is the chosen product name; **eventcard.cc** is the chosen domain. Domain registration, ownership, and deployment have not been established.

Build an invitation maker for **weddings and birthday parties** first. People can choose a styled template, personalize it, and preview it without an account. After finishing, they are prompted to sign up or sign in and pay before sharing. The product must provide both a hosted invitation webpage link and a downloadable image. The first release should center on this flow while leaving room to add features later.

Paid owners can edit without an edit-count limit and switch templates without losing entered event details. Edits update the webpage at the same link; previously downloaded images stay static and must be regenerated and downloaded again.

A freeform canvas where users draw and animate invitations was considered and set aside because its development cost was too high for the current plan.

## Aspirations, not validated claims

- A modern, creative visual style.
- Birthday support alongside weddings.
- Broad language coverage, with an ambition of 30+ major languages for both the app interface and invitations. The exact set and rollout are undecided.

These are intended ways to stand out, not evidence of market demand or a proven advantage. Audience, geography, payment packages, and business model remain open.
