# Reference notes

## ChungDoi public website — checked 2026-10-04

Sources: [home](https://chungdoi.com/ru), [templates](https://chungdoi.com/ru/templates), [wedding invitation guide](https://chungdoi.com/ru/create-wedding-invitation), [pricing](https://chungdoi.com/ru/pricing). These are public-site observations, not an editor hands-on test or a product requirement.

- The site focuses on weddings and presents a template → details/photos → share flow. It advertises guest management, personal guest links and RSVP, maps, photos, gift QR, multilingual invitations, and video/image downloads.
- It says content is preserved when switching templates and previously shared links show updated invitations after edits.
- Its pricing page currently advertises US$19.99 as a one-time payment per invitation, a three-day free trial with up to 20 guests, and unlimited guests after payment. Prices and terms can change.
- The home page currently lists 16 invitation languages, including Uzbek. An earlier research note said 15 and did not list Uzbek; the live page checked on this date supersedes that note.
- The user finds the competitor name unclear and hard to remember. This is a subjective reaction, not a validated market finding.

The public pages did not establish how the editor works in practice. Do not infer implementation details or assume feature parity is needed.
