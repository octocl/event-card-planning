# MVP scope

## Confirmed core flow

1. Choose a styled wedding or birthday template.
2. Personalize the invitation.
3. Preview the result without signing up.
4. After finishing, sign up or sign in and pay, then share a hosted webpage link and download an image.

Owners of paid invitations can edit without an edit-count limit and switch templates while keeping entered event details. The shared webpage updates at the same link. Downloaded images are static; edits require regenerating and downloading a new image.

The exact editable fields, number and style of launch templates, image format and dimensions, and relationship between the webpage and image layouts are open questions. The payment packages and whether one payment unlocks both outputs are deferred to pricing discussion. These will be worked through in the interactive planning wayfinder session.

## Future possibilities, not MVP commitments

Guest management, RSVP, music, video export, and gift QR codes are examples for later consideration. No commitment has been made to include them, or to match any competitor's feature set.

Language support should be planned from the start. The 30+ language ambition covers both the app interface and invitations, but the exact languages and MVP launch count remain undecided.
